Manual image upload

Upload a RAR or ZIP archive of NMW images for processing

Normally the survey images are uploaded automatically by astrocam-go running on the telescope control computer. An archive of images may also be uploaded manually through this page if needed for testing.

It will take a long time to upload and process the data. Please be patient and hope for the best.
