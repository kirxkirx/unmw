NMW transient search, image archive and photometry

New Milky Way survey data access

Transient candidate lists and processing logs

Per-night pages listing the candidate transients found in each processed field, together with the processing logs.

Monitored sources

Lightcurves of the sources in the monitoring list, updated after every processed image of their fields.

Forced photometry on recent images

Measure the brightness at any sky position across all images taken during the last week. The recent images are used as they come: some frames may be affected by clouds.

Forced photometry on the archived images

Measure the brightness at any sky position across the long-term image archive. The archive is expected to include only good-quality frames.

Reference images

Browse all reference images, or find which fields cover a particular sky position.

Manually upload an archive of images for processing (rarely needed - normally the survey images are uploaded automatically by the telescope control computer).
